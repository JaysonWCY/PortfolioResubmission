Advanced Persistent Threats: Applying the Cyber Kill Chain Model to a Case Study

Vulnerability Analysis – Literature Review Activity

Using the website(s) assigned to you in Unit 1, carry out the following exercise and answer the questions listed below. Instructions Based on the information identified about your assigned websites, carry out a literature search/ audit on software sites and the national vulnerabilities database to create a baseline audit on potential vulnerabilities with websites.

RESPONSE

My chosen website is: https://hack-yourself-first.com/ It is a car voting website where users can vote on their favourite cars. The website doesn’t have any payment function so any vulnerability protection regarding that is not required. They do however store user details such as emails and passwords. These will require hashing in storage and encrypted transmission. This website also has a forum where you can post comments and hold discussions. This will require input validations and checks. Other than that, it will require the baseline standards of HTTPS regulations as well proper error handling as seen in the GDPR guidance (I (Legislative acts) REGULATIONS REGULATION (EU) 2016/679 OF THE EUROPEAN PARLIAMENT AND OF THE COUNCIL of 27 April 2016 on the protection of natural persons with regard to the processing of personal data and on the free movement of such data, and repealing Directive 95/46/EC (General Data Protection Regulation) (Text with EEA relevance), no date). To test these methods, Kali Linux can be used to send requests to the server as well as check error handling as such. However, things like hashing passwords and most often can only be checked through Whitebox testing and looking at the code and database itself. While finding the literature and databases to create a baseline security requirement wasn’t extreme complicated, interpreting and deciding which to apply was more of the challenge. With the existence of multiple frameworks such as GDPR, the OECD Privacy Guidelines, and PDPA, it is easy to overlook specific policies that are required to be fulfilled. Having multiple standards make it harder to know which ones exactly have to be followed, this causes more of a possibility of overlooking certain guidelines that would have been applicable to the circumstance. With standards also being updated constantly, it becomes increasingly difficult for organizations and researchers to remain fully compliant. The most viable way forward is to meet as many of these requirements as possible while remaining vigilant to ensure the highest level of protection. This may affect the report, as there is a possibility that certain vulnerabilities could be overlooked during the review process or possibly not assessed with enough depth.
