Collaborative Discussion 1: Digitalisation – What are the Security Implications of the Digital Economy?

Read Doroiman, M. M. and Sîrghi, N. (2024) and then post a message that reflects your thoughts and responses to the questions below. You should support your arguments with appropriate academic references. In your responses, consider the following questions:

  • What do you consider as a fully digital enterprise?
  • What are the cyber security challenges/concerns with a fully digital enterprise?
  • What are the cyber security challenges for a bricks and mortar SME wanting to become a digital enterprise?

RESPONSE

A fully digitalised enterprise is one where all aspects of day-to-day operations as well major projects use digital technology where possible. These enterprises transfer data from one digital interface to as opposed to physical documents and the such. The company itself also tends to interact with their customers, suppliers, and other businesses mostly online. They also tend to adopt advanced methods of data storage and manipulation, such as AI and cloud storage. They would try their best to leverage technology to improve efficiency as best as possible. Some challenges with going fully digital are mostly regarding the leaking and stealing of data, or the interruption of service. This concerns threats such as phishing, ransomware, and inadequate security safeguards. Another main concern are the regulations that have to be followed, set by regulating boards, regarding sensitive data such as user data. When an SME decides to become digital, the main challenges are mostly regarding adapting to the new system, as well as transferring data. The currently employees have to be trained on how to use, maintain and repair the system, along many new workflows that come with digitalisation. This normally comes with a dedicated IT team to help the employees adjust to their new workflow and/or take some of the complications that come with digitalisation. These all comes with costs that SME’s can’t afford to be burdened with as opposed to large companies, with bigger budgets.

Summary

The definition of a digital enterprise was common among all peers,

The definition of a digital enterprise was common among all peers, where most if not all processes in a company would be digital in some form or another. This would be done to increase efficiency and improve workflow while also opening more opportunities into the wider audience.

Many cybersecurity concerns were raised such as phishing, Distributed Denial-of-Service (DDoS) attacks and ransomware to name a few. These all follow the theme that I have stated where most concerns are regarding either the leaking of data or the interruption of service.

A lot of my peers had the same challenges for an SME wanting to become digital. These were all regarding resource constraints. The constraints are such as:

  • Money – As it takes a lot of money to purchase the components of a new system
  • Time – As adoption of a new system most likely means the slowing down or halting of current business.
  • Employee skills – Current employees have to be trained as well as needed the time to gain enough expertise in the system for it be actually be considered efficient.
